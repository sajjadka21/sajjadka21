# Hi, I'm Sajjad Kazemi

I build practical software across **applied AI, data, and product engineering**. I am pursuing an M.Sc. in **Artificial Intelligence and Robotics** at the University of Science and Technology of Mazandaran.

My work brings research ideas into usable systems—from language and data pipelines to geospatial applications and cross-platform tools.

### Selected projects

- **[Ratatoskr](https://github.com/sajjadka21/ratatoskr)** — a fast, local-first, Persian-first download manager for Windows and Android.
- **[ParsYar Demo](https://github.com/sajjadka21/parsyar-demo)** — a Persian, right-to-left showcase of a gym-management dashboard, with fictional data. [Visit the live ParsYar product](https://parsyar.hivenest.ir).
- **[LEAF](https://github.com/sajjadka21/saeed-leaf-table2text)** — a logic- and evidence-aware table-to-text research pipeline.
